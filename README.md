# Hi, I'm Jose 👋

I'm from Quilpué, Chile 🇨🇱, and my interests bring together technology, design, and specialty coffee.

I enjoy building websites, experimenting with Linux, and making everyday tools feel more personal and useful. I'm drawn to how things work, how they look, and how people experience them.

I've contributed to websites for Multidiversos ONG and Imperio TEA, supporting their work around disability awareness and social inclusion.

This space is where I share projects, explore ideas, and learn by building.
